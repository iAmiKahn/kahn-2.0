# Valuation Methodology

Last updated: May 2026

## Comp Selection
- Prefer sales closed within 90 days; extend to 6 months if thin.
- Same submarket/school district; within ~1 mile urban, wider rural.
- Within ~20% of subject's gross living area (residential).
- Match property type: do not comp land against improved parcels.

## Adjustments
- Adjust comps toward the subject for: GLA, lot size, condition,
  bed/bath count, garage, age, and dated vs. updated finish.
- State each adjustment as a dollar figure with brief reasoning.

## Confidence Range
- Report a low/high range, not a single number.
- Tighten the range when 4+ strong recent comps agree.
- Widen it for unique properties, thin comps, or volatile markets.

## Land Parcels
- Value on price per acre / per buildable lot from land comps only.
- Note zoning, utilities access, and developability — they swing value.

## Commercial
- Note that income approach (cap rate) often governs; flag when an
  income analysis is needed and what data is missing.

## Multiple Parcels
- Sum-of-parts: value each parcel independently, then total.
- Assemblage: if combined parcels enable a use neither allows alone,
  note potential assemblage premium and that it requires market support.
