# PitchBlack Intake Questionnaire

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-17
**Purpose:** Generalized intake questionnaire deployed after the Client Onboarding Script walkthrough. Completion unlocks the proposal + build timeline. Vertical-agnostic; designed for small-batch food/beverage and adjacent small-business verticals.

**Deployment:** Typeform, Airtable Form, Google Form, or Notion form. Estimated completion time: **30 minutes.**

**Links from:** [CLIENT_ONBOARDING_SCRIPT.md](CLIENT_ONBOARDING_SCRIPT.md) CTA.

## Cover page copy (what the prospect sees before starting)

> **Welcome.** This questionnaire has 15 questions and takes about 30 minutes. Answer thoroughly; the quality of what we build is downstream of the quality of your answers.
>
> Sections:
> 1. About your business (5 questions) — the shape of your operation.
> 2. Existing tools (4 questions) — what you're already running.
> 3. Brand + voice (3 questions) — what you sound like.
> 4. Scope + priorities (2 questions) — what matters most to build first.
> 5. Logistics (1 question) — when you want to start.
>
> You can save and come back. When you submit, we review within 2 business days and reply with a specific proposal.

## Deployment notes

### Form platform recommendation

**Tally** or **Airtable Forms** for form hosting; both support conditional branching, file uploads, and webhook-to-HighLevel integration (so a completed questionnaire auto-creates a Contact + Opportunity in Dan's Agency sub-account).

### Length tuning

Current structure = ~15 questions across 5 sections. Post-deployment data may show drop-off — if so, trim §3.2 (brand voice) to a shorter prompt and move §3.3 (menu) to a post-submission follow-up. Do NOT trim §1.1 (legal entity) or §2 (tools) — those directly drive build design.

### Data retention

Completed questionnaires get stored in the PitchBlack Agency's internal Airtable or a dedicated Notion page. Contains PII (EIN, contact details, business address) — treat as confidential. Purge at 12 months if the prospect doesn't convert.
